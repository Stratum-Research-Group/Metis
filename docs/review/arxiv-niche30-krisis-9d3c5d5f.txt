KRISIS - NICHE PICK FROM THE 3-MONTH SCREEN (1 of the top 30)

1. Where Does Retrieval-Based Open-Ended Evaluation Fail?
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30467
Stress-tests decompose-then-verify factuality pipelines in open-ended medical QA: best
verifier recall of clinician-flagged errors was 34.9% even with gold evidence guaranteed
present, persisting across scale, reasoning effort and fine-tuning - their evidence the
failure is structural. Produces a taxonomy splitting retrieval-stage from
verification-stage failures, and separates "comprehensiveness" (evidence may not exist)
from "identifiability" (it exists but is buried).
Practical reach: if Krisis grows its claim-level support verifier, this maps its failure
surface. Under a binary supported/unsupported policy, "claim false" and "evidence missing"
collapse into one outcome - abstention only helps if the verifier can RECOGNIZE
insufficient evidence, and the verification-stage categories are exactly the ways that
recognition fails. A ready-made fixture list for the abstain path.
Honest limit: medical domain; the taxonomy is induced with human-in-the-loop LLM judging
(the judge is the class of system under study - they disclose this). Numbers are their
setting; the reporting shape is portable.
