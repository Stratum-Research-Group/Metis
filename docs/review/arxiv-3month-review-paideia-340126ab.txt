1. Latent On-Policy Self-Distillation (LOPD)
arXiv:2608.13040 (13 Aug 2026), Zhang, Lyu, Sun, Yu, Zhao, Ren, Yan - NUS / BUPT / SJTU
Coverage: PDF downloaded and read (abstract, method overview, main-results tables and
prose); training-detail level at claim level.

WHAT IT IS / SHOWED
On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) uses a privileged self-teacher to give dense supervision
on the student's own trajectories, but existing methods need designer-specified privileged
artifacts (answers, feedback, skills). LOPD makes the privileged context LEARNABLE: a
composer transforms retrieved experience into compact continuous tokens; because that
context is differentiable, the same training process improves both student and composer. At
inference only the student is deployed.
Author-reported results: best aggregate in all ten backbone-benchmark comparisons across
three model backbones and seven benchmarks (tool use: EnvScaler, BFCL-v3, ACEBench; code:
LiveCodeBench, EvalPlus). On EnvScaler with Qwen3-4B, LOPD 63.7 vs strongest competing
method GRPO 61.8 (their Table 1). Code: Qwen3-4B LiveCodeBench/EvalPlus aggregates
48.78/81.36; OLMo3-7B 50.98/78.41 (+2.69/+0.55 over the strongest alternatives). With
Qwen3-8B, LOPD exceeds OPSD/SDFT/Skill-SD by 14.4/10.2/6.2 points on the tool-use
aggregate. They also report matching or beating GRPO and Skill-SD while using less than
30% of their ROLLOUT BUDGET - that is rollout count in their training setup, not total
training compute or memory; it says nothing about whether the method fits a 16GB card.
Their own honesty note: adding privileged information does not ALWAYS improve on the
vanilla model - no hand-crafted context is universally optimal; that is their motivation
for learning the context instead.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR PAIDEIA (my judgment)
- Self-distillation with a learnable teacher context is a credible middle path for the
  walk-policy/router training paideia is meant to own: denser supervision than pure GRPO
  without hand-built privileged labels. Note the comparison is against RLVR/OPSD/GRPO-style
  baselines on their seven benchmarks - nothing here validates it for graph-walk policies
  specifically, and the rollout-budget saving does not translate into a feasibility claim
  for the home rig.
- Code: official repo verified resolving - https://github.com/bingreeky/LOPD (Python,
  created 13 Aug 2026). The paper also advertises Hugging Face checkpoints
  (Qwen3-8B-LOPD, Olmo3-7B-LOPD) but I could not resolve the HF org, so checkpoint
  availability is unverified.
- Sequencing note tied to the 3 Nov review state: this is a methods lead for when paideia's
  training path actually trains (real training currently raises TrainingUnavailableError
  with the default explicitly marked SIMULATED) - the prerequisite is a working training
  backend, not a better objective.

2. K-OPSD: Verifiable On-Policy Self-Distillation for Post-Training VLMs on AEC Drawings
   (brief, applied variant)
arXiv:2609.34082 (28 Sep 2026), Bai, Chionna, Singh KC, Amol, Tinnemeyer - AWS
Coverage: PDF downloaded and read (abstract and method summary only); results not reviewed.

LEAD ONLY - abstract/method-level: OPSD with VERIFIABLE supervision - the teacher is built
from the model's own best-of-N generations, certified by a process-level verifier; failed
prompts are rescued by resampling under a hint that exposes the verified answer. Domain is
architecture/engineering/construction drawing understanding, so the method transfer is the
point, not the domain: the "verifier-certified self-teacher + hint-rescue" pattern is a
concrete answer to where privileged context comes from when you have a checker but no
labels - the situation paideia's GRPO/self-distillation path would be in if it can get
verifiable rewards from dianoia/krisis.
