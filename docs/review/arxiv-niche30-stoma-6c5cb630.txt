STOMA - NICHE PICKS FROM THE 3-MONTH SCREEN (2 of the top 30)

1. Document Retrieval-Aware Chunking (D-RAC)
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24220
Normalize office-style inputs (DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, scans, native PDF) to PDF first, then
convert pages to retrieval-optimized Markdown (tables become self-contained prose) and
chunk. The bet is that many formats have a deterministic PDF rendering - though rendering
can lose information (a spreadsheet's off-screen sheets, embedded media), so what survives
normalization has to be checked per format. Their numbers, on their corpus: processing
completed in 72 minutes with no failed documents, and 95.7% fewer chunking-stage output
tokens than agentic chunking with frontier LLMs.
Practical reach: one deterministic rendering target instead of N format-specific parsers
fits Stoma's fail-closed bar - normalization failures become detectable at the rendering
boundary, before parsing. The table-to-prose normalization is a specific chunking choice
to evaluate against LTHCM retrieval quality rather than assume.
Honest limit: the zero-failure figure is their corpus and their pipeline, not a universal
property. Camera photos (a Stoma input) are not documents with a faithful PDF rendering,
so this covers part of the input space. Cost figures are their enterprise-API context.

2. Jina-OCR-v1: Efficient Document Parsing with Speculative Decoding and Dense Verifiable
   Rewards
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03181
Document parsing aimed at low-budget serving: compressed vision encoder + 3B MoE decoder
(~570M active parameters per token), a speculative decoding head keeping decoding
lossless, post-trained with DENSE VERIFIABLE REWARDS - deterministic
formula/table/structure checks with partial credit. Author-reported: 91.14 OmniDocBench,
2.57 pages/s, the highest throughput in their comparison, measured on an NVIDIA L4.
Practical reach: a candidate parsing component for Stoma's document path with published
throughput in a datacenter-lite setup. Note that ~570M ACTIVE parameters per token says
nothing by itself about resident memory (the full 3B MoE plus encoder must fit) or about
throughput on your card - local fit and speed need checking before adoption. The
dense-verifiable-rewards pattern (deterministic structural checks, partial credit) is
also Dokime/Paideia material for a parsing-quality eval track.
Honest limit: manufacturer-reported numbers in their setup; weights availability and
license not verified.
