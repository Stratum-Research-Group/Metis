   Location: src/dokime/compare.py:293-334.

Refuse the comparison with a clear CompareError naming the query and models if either hash is null/empty or they differ. Add regression tests: one model has a null-hash failed row and the other has a scored, hashed row; both have at least one otherwise valid complete pair.
