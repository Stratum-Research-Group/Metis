1. MI-MIDI: Mechanistic Interpretability of Text-to-MIDI Generation Models via Probing,
   Lenses and Steering
arXiv:2608.06638 (6 Aug 2026), Pocwiardowski & Modrzejewski - Warsaw University of Technology
Coverage: PDF downloaded and read (abstract, methods summary, headline findings).

WHAT IT IS / SHOWED
Mechanistic interpretability of SYMBOLIC music generation (the field has concentrated on
audio models). Two public text-to-MIDI systems of contrasting design: the purpose-built
encoder-decoder text2midi, and MIDI-LLM (Llama 3.2 1B extended with MIDI tokens). Methods:
linear probing, logit and tuned lenses, activation patching, difference-in-means steering.
Findings (author-reported): pitch, instrumentation, harmony and texture are linearly
decodable in both models; the two architectures form that structure differently - text2midi
refines predictions gradually across depth, while MIDI-LLM works largely in its inherited
TEXTUAL basis before a sharp late rotation into the musical vocabulary, with patching
showing a matching late attenuation of prompt-driven instrument transfer. Steering produces
bidirectional changes in register and polyphony in both systems, and tempo/energy in
MIDI-LLM; their two-orientation protocol separates directional control from noise.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR DIOPTRA (my judgment)
- A compact reference pipeline for Dioptra's evidence-record design: probe -> lens ->
  patch -> steer on the SAME phenomenon, with each method's output logged so conclusions
  cite which method saw what. Their contrast structure (two architectures, same analyses)
  is also the right template for Dioptra comparison reports: the interesting output is not
  "structure exists" but WHERE and HOW differently it forms per architecture.
- Concrete caveat worth copying into Dioptra's method library: their two-orientation
  steering protocol exists because steering vectors can look effective through
  distributional side effects; any Dioptra steering feature needs an equivalent control.
- KraftBeat cross-reference (flagged, not developed here): this is direct evidence about
  what text-to-MIDI models expose for control - relevant if KraftBeat's MIDI path ever
  wants steering-level control rather than prompt-level control.
