KYBERNETES - takeaways from the October 2026 arXiv sweep
2026-10-31

Source: screen of the newest ~200 arXiv papers (AI/ML plus project-relevant). 12 papers got deeper section-level reads; 27 got standard reviews with varying coverage - entries reflect that coverage, not full-paper claims. Read depth is not a priority ranking.
Numbering: [n] is the paper's number in the sweep list sent in chat on 2026-10-31.
Status: research takeaways for review. Not implementation specs, not commitments. Papers that fit other projects have their own txt files.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[5] Don't Lose the Thread: Empowering Long-Horizon LLM Agents with Cognitive Resource Self-Allocation (CORAL)
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40350
Fit: STRONG - agent control is Kybernetes' orchestration territory.
Take: checkpointed working memory and deliberate context resets for long-running tasks. The fit here is stronger than any claim that it directly improves Dianoia's graph walk (see the Dianoia txt).
Constraint: keep two contributions separate - the memory procedure and the distilled SFT. The headline gains come mostly from the distillation; that training arm would live in Paideia.

[19] VeriCUA: Verification as Agentic Exploration for Computer-Using Agents
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40578
Fit: STRONG - Kybernetes traces are the natural subject.
Take: a verifier that actively checks unresolved requirements using GUI or code tools, for when checking an outcome needs more than reading the agent's answer. The evaluation harness itself is Dokime's - the metric entry is in the Dokime txt.

[7] Prefix Executability: Evaluating Tool-Using Agents Beyond Final Success
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40444
Fit: CROSS-REFERENCE.
Take: Kybernetes traces benefit from step-level executability grading - valid intermediate actions, not just final success. Dokime owns the metric; full entry in the Dokime txt.

[6] Understanding and Mitigating Role Spoofing in LLMs
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40396
Fit: BOUNDARY - the execution path is an enforcement point.
Take: Kybernetes handles external content when executing; the authority boundary has to be enforced outside the model. Threat model and policy home are in the Phylax txt.
