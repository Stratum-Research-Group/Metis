DIOPTRA - takeaways from the October 2026 arXiv sweep
2026-10-31

Source: screen of the newest ~200 arXiv papers (AI/ML plus project-relevant). 12 papers got deeper section-level reads; 27 got standard reviews with varying coverage - entries reflect that coverage, not full-paper claims. Read depth is not a priority ranking.
Numbering: [n] is the paper's number in the sweep list sent in chat on 2026-10-31.
Status: research takeaways for review. Not implementation specs, not commitments. Papers that fit other projects have their own txt files.
Dioptra stays a standalone workbench with a versioned Metis integration; nothing here changes that. The reasoning-geometry side project rides along in this file, labelled per entry.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[12] One Vector or Many? Clustered Optimal-Transport Steering under Quantization  [reasoning-geometry side project]
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40055
Fit: CLOSE - direct test bed for the "sectors within the vectors" idea.
Take: the comparison between one mean steering vector and a selectively routed clustered map is the right baseline structure for the sectors experiments - test the routed map against the simpler single vector before crediting the sectors.
Constraint: their quantization tests were INT8/NF4, not your GGUF setup - that transfer remains untested. If a steered model is ever served, the serving side is noted in the Hestia txt.

[37] Factorized Representations of Concurrent Temporal Variables in Pretrained Language Models  [reasoning-geometry side project]
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40328
Fit: CLOSE - methodology template.
Take: steer and ablate subspaces to test whether independently changing variables have separable representations. Directly useful methodology for the sectors idea - without assuming those sectors already exist as you envisage them.

[18] Pre-Crime for Agents, at the Last Second: Probing When a Recruited Agent Gives In  [Dioptra workbench]
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40588
Fit: STRONG - linear probes on activations are Dioptra's toolkit.
Take: activation probes that flag an impending secret-leaking tool call before it fires. Dioptra is where such a probe gets built and tested; Phylax is where it would run as a monitor (see the Phylax txt).
Constraint: the paper does not establish superiority over a properly trained text-only monitor.

[13] world2vec: Mechanistic World Models through Latent Dynamics Learning  [research direction]
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40637
Fit: EXPLORATORY.
Take: "latent faithfulness as a measurable property" - check learned latents against known true dynamics. A probe design pattern.
Constraint: the experiments are 263k-parameter world models. They do not establish anything about LLM-scale representations.

[14] Probing Spatial State Representations in Visual Foundation Models  [research direction]
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40636
Fit: CONDITIONAL - methodology only.
Take: a four-level probing battery (structure, persistence, prediction, actionability). Worth borrowing if the workbench ever extends to visual or spatial models. Their finding that no single representation covers everything is a useful caution for probe design generally.

[2] Rethinking Neural Thickets Without Weight Perturbations  [reasoning-geometry side project]
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40649
Fit: MODERATION for the geometry work.
Take: before crediting weight-space geometry or sophisticated selection, test whether ordinary diversity plus voting explains the improvement. The selection-layer entry is in the Krisis txt.
