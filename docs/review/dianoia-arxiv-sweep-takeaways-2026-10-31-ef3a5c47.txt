[5] Don't Lose the Thread: Empowering Long-Horizon LLM Agents with Cognitive Resource Self-Allocation (CORAL)
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40350
Fit: MODERATE - no established direct improvement to the graph walk.
Take: if anything transfers, it is checkpoint/reset discipline for very long walks. The stronger agent-control fit is Kybernetes' (see the Kybernetes txt), and the headline gains come mostly from distilled SFT (the training arm would live in Paideia).

[28] Algebraic World Model: From Exploration to Proofs
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40503
Fit: EXPLORATORY - a formal-verification direction.
Take: turn learned rules into executable hypotheses and kernel-checked proofs (Lean 4), including impossibility results. Interesting for checking consequences and contradictions of a learned model - adjacent to Dianoia's contradiction-checking ambitions.
Constraint: a proof is only as applicable as the learned model's correspondence to the real problem. This is a research direction, not a component.

[7] Prefix Executability: Evaluating Tool-Using Agents Beyond Final Success
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40444
Fit: CROSS-REFERENCE.
Take: walk traces benefit from step-level executability grading - valid EXPAND/BACKTRACK/ANSWER steps, not just a correct final answer. Dokime owns the metric; full entry in the Dokime txt.
