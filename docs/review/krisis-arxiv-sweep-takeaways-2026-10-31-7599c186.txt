[2] Rethinking Neural Thickets Without Weight Perturbations
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40649
Fit: STRONG, with a boundary.
Take: ensemble/voting behaviour without weight perturbation is a selection-layer question - candidate diversity plus a simple vote is the baseline Krisis should beat before anything fancier gets credit.
Constraint: the paper's voting scheme is not a drop-in for Krisis. Krisis is deterministic selection with abstention and a claim-level support verifier; any voting idea has to respect that contract and fail closed.

[11] Judged in Company: In-Context Batching Makes LLM Verdicts Depend on Other Items
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40241
Fit: CONDITIONAL - relevant if Krisis ever uses LLM judging in its verifier path.
Take: batching several claims into one judge prompt lets neighbours change verdicts. Keep single-item judging as the baseline and measure batching stability separately. The harness-level entry is in the Dokime txt.
