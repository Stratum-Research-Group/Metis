[6] Understanding and Mitigating Role Spoofing in LLMs
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40396
Fit: STRONG - the threat model belongs to Phylax.
Take: document or tool content impersonating a higher-authority message is a distinct attack surface for any agent that reads external content. Phylax is the natural policy home; the enforcement points are the ingestion path (Stoma) and the execution path (Kybernetes).
Constraint: a model's own claim that it knows who said what is not sufficient protection - the boundary has to be enforced outside the model.

[15] ShallowDefend: Harnessing Shallow Safety Alignment for Multi-Turn Defender
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40627
Fit: STRONG - a concrete policy pattern.
Take: make the safety decision an explicit, parseable token span (Assess, Decide, Respond), trained with RL on a verifiable reward. Decision first, answer second, with labels you can check - a candidate pattern for Phylax's policy layer. Not a replacement for external enforcement.
Evidence: their numbers are in-distribution to their replayed attack set.
Note: the training arm of this pattern would live in Paideia.

[18] Pre-Crime for Agents, at the Last Second
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40588
Fit: POSSIBLE MONITORING HOME.
Take: activation probes that flag an impending secret-leaking tool call before it fires. Building and testing such a probe is Dioptra work (see the Dioptra txt); if it proves out, running it as a monitor is Phylax's.
Constraint: not established as better than a properly trained text-only monitor.
