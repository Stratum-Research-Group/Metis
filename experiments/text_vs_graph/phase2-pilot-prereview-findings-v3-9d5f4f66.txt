PHASE 2 PILOT PRE-REVIEW - EVIDENCE AUDIT v3 FINAL (Instinct, 2026-10-30)
Audited: pilot report (markdown, line refs "L#") against three raw logs: the Phase 2
extraction log (41 records), the Phase 2 QA log (656 records), and the recovered Phase 1
confirmatory results log (10,800 records). Method: recomputed every checkable aggregate and
bootstrap from the raw logs; no code changes, no inference run, no source edits.

VERDICT: The numbers reconcile end to end. Every Phase 2 aggregate matches the raw logs
exactly over the report's stated 40-story scope, and every Phase 1 comparison figure the
report quotes (all 9 subset accuracies, the Arm A per-question column, all five cross-run
bootstrap rows) now verifies exactly against the recovered Phase 1 raw log. Grading integrity
is clean in both phases. The real problems are failure attribution: the report's central Q3
narrative ("the model struggles with multi-hop ablation") is built on rows that
overwhelmingly show an output-truncation pattern at exactly 512 tokens - in BOTH phases -
plus undisclosed generation settings, a 41-story/40-story scope discrepancy, and two
misstated extraction stats. Do not lock Section 6 (L115-126) into the architecture docs
until findings 1-6 are settled.

NUMBERED FINDINGS

1. SCOPE DISCREPANCY - the supplied Phase 2 logs contain 41 stories; the report freezes 40
   (L8, L44). Extraction log: conf_s001-conf_s041 (seeds 4001-4041), 41 records. QA log:
   656 rows = 41 stories x 4 arms (B, C, D, F) x 4 questions. Report scope (L8): 40 stories
   (conf_s001-conf_s040, seeds 4001-4040), 640 evaluated responses + 40 extraction passes =
   680 invocations. Every report aggregate matches the 40-story subset exactly, so the report
   is internally consistent on 40 stories - but the data also contains conf_s041 / seed 4041
   (all 16 QA rows correct, extraction fidelity 1.0). This may simply be a live snapshot
   taken after the report froze its 40-story scope. A frozen 40-story pilot with conf_s041
   assigned to the remaining run (L132: conf_s041-conf_s300) is a valid partition - but the
   report, the logs and the full-run story list should be aligned on which scope was actually
   run, and the already-computed conf_s041 results reused rather than re-run.

2. ARM B AND C FAILURES CLUSTER AT EXACTLY 512 OUTPUT TOKENS - strong truncation evidence,
   configured cap not proven. Observed: across 164 rows each, arms B and C never exceed 512
   tokens_out (B max = 512 exactly, 1 row; C max = 512 exactly, 3 rows). Every row pinned at
   512 is a parse failure scored wrong, each raw ending mid-sentence:
     - conf_s002_q3 C (512 tok), conf_s011_q3 C (512), conf_s032_q3 C (512): 3 of Arm C's
       3 total errors (157/160).
     - conf_s037_q3 B (512).
   The Phase 1 raw log shows the same ceiling: arms A/B/C max at exactly 512 tokens_out
   (verified again here; Arm A global max = 512), and the earlier Phase 1 audit found 99.2%
   of its parse failures at >=448 tokens. Exact clustering at one value plus mid-sentence
   endings strongly supports output truncation at or near 512 tokens on the single-pass
   arms - but neither phase's artifacts include request settings or finish_reason fields,
   so the configured limit itself is not proven. The generation settings requested in the
   Phase 1 audit (frozen prompts, per-arm max_tokens, temperature, stop tokens) are still
   not in evidence.

3. THE INHERITED ARM A BASELINE SHOWS THE SAME PATTERN - all 7 of its Q3 errors sit at the
   512 ceiling. From the recovered Phase 1 log (level 0.0, conf_s001-s040): Arm A's Q3 errors
   are conf_s002, conf_s018, conf_s027, conf_s029, conf_s030, conf_s037, conf_s040 - all
   parse_ok=false, all tokens_out=512, all raws ending mid-sentence. conf_s037_q3 was cut
   off while emitting a final answer JSON matching the gold set. Arm A's only Q2 error
   (conf_s032_q2) and one of its two Q4 errors (conf_s006_q4) show the same pattern.
   Consequence for the report's framing: L26-31, L85 and L89 present Q3 as the model
   "struggling to follow multi-hop parent ablation" on pure text (Arm A 82.5% vs B/C 92.5%).
   In the underlying data, both sides of that comparison have error sets dominated by the
   512-token truncation pattern, not by completed-but-wrong reasoning. The direction of the
   Q3 gap is consistent with the data; its measured size conflates reasoning difficulty with
   output length. No counterfactual claim is possible from this data about what any arm would
   score with a larger budget - that needs a consistently applied rerun.

4. OBSERVED PHASE 2 FAILURE COMPOSITION OVER 40 STORIES (12 wrong rows of 640):
   - 10 output-level failures, all Q3 except one: the four 512-token rows in finding 2;
     four Arm D rows whose raws also end mid-sentence (conf_s002_q3 592 tok, conf_s004_q3
     560, conf_s030_q3 550, conf_s037_q3 560); and two degenerate 4-token outputs
     (conf_s023_q2 and conf_s023_q3, arm B: the entire raw is "<|channel>thought<tool_call|>" -
     the model opened a tool-call channel and stopped).
   - 1 row with template contamination: conf_s031_q3 arm D - the raw output contains a
     fabricated continuation: a literal "<|turn>user" marker, an invented next question
     ("What is the start time of Event E6?"), "<|turn>model", and an answer to the invented
     question. This row was scored wrong (parsed [] vs gold [E3,E4,E5,E8]) - one score
     failure coincided with this contamination; whether the contamination caused the score
   is not established.
   - 1 wrong answer in an otherwise complete response: conf_s028_q3 arm B over-propagated
     the ablation (answered [E4,E5,E8], gold [E5,E8]; extraction fidelity 1.0 on that story).
   Note the recurrence: conf_s002, conf_s030 and conf_s037 fail across multiple arms and both
   phases, always with the truncation pattern - the failures concentrate on the same
   long-reasoning stories.

5. MULTI-TURN CONTAINMENT FAILURE BEYOND THAT ONE ROW. 8 of 656 QA rows contain a literal
   "<|turn>" marker with a fabricated conversation continuation - all on Q3, all in the
   two-pass arms (D x3, F x5); 5 land in pass 1, 3 in the final raw. Separately, 627/656 QA
   raws and all 41 extraction graphs begin with a stray "<|channel>thought" marker, with
   malformed variants observed ("<|channel>thoughtthought", "<|channel>thought_thought",
   "<|thought|>"), and 171 pass-1 raws carry long reasoning text after the thought marker.
   The parser absorbed almost all of this, but it is template fragility in the pinned
   abliterated model that the report's clean 680-invocation framing (L8) does not mention.

6. TWO-PASS TOKEN ACCOUNTING STILL UNRESOLVED - D/F exceed 512, cause not established.
   Arm D tokens_out max = 592, Arm F max = 652, vs the 512 ceiling on B/C. Phase 1 showed
   the same shape (D/F up to 762). This is consistent with token counts summed across the
   two passes as planned, and does NOT by itself establish unequal output budgets across
   arms - but without the per-pass request settings, neither explanation can be confirmed.

8. EXTRACTION LATENCY AND OUTPUT-TOKEN FIGURES DO NOT MATCH THE DATA (L55, L126).
   Report: "Mean Extractor Latency 6.78s / story, ~870 tokens in, ~590 tokens out".
   Data (40-story scope): mean 5.75s (median 5.89, range 4.95-9.01); 873.5 tokens in
   (consistent with ~870); 553.4 tokens out (median 567.5, range 490-674) - not ~590.
   Over all 41 stories: 5.83s, 873.3 in, 554.3 out. The reported 6.78s matches no statistic
   of either scope.

9. OMISSIONS ARE POSITIONALLY CLUSTERED - 8 of 12 missing events are E2. Observed missing
   events: E2 x8, E8 x2, E5 x1, E6 x1 (L51's total of 12 reconciles). The report attributes
   omissions "almost exclusively" to non-standard formatting tokens such as start_hour=_28_
   (L22, L57-59). Partially visible in the data: underscore-wrapped tokens (_28_, _55_, _4_,
   ...) appear in 11 extracted graphs, and 9 of those 11 stories had an omission - but 2

   extracted graph, and the story prose is not in the attachment set, so the causal
   attribution cannot be fully verified. The E2 clustering (67% of omissions) is not
   mentioned in the report and is worth isolating against the story template before the
   full run.
