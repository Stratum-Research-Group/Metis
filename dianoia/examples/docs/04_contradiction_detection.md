# Sheaf-Theoretic Consistency and Verification

When multiple evidence passages are retrieved or an answer is drafted, semantic contradictions may arise. Traditional RAG systems often produce hallucinations that directly contradict their source material.

Sheaf theory provides a mathematical foundation for local-to-global consistency:
- Sections over open sets represent local statements or truth claims.
- Restriction maps project statements across overlapping semantic domains.
- Cohomological obstructions quantify global inconsistency: non-zero obstruction energy directly measures contradiction.
- Dianoia applies answer-vs-evidence verification to verify that synthesized answers do not contradict the retrieved evidence before finalizing output.
